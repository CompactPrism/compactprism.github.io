How it was made

No cameras, no stock footage, no image or video generators. Every pixel is computed by React components, GLSL shaders and GPU particle systems; every note of the score is synthesised in Python; the narration is an open-source voice. Claude wrote all of it, in Claude Code, coordinating a team of agents for research, voice, music and scenes.

01

Script & fact-check

Every date, number and quote on screen was checked against primary or reliable sources before a frame was drawn.

02

Voice

Narration synthesised with Kokoro-82M, an open-source text-to-speech model, running locally.

03

Score

An orchestral-electronic score built from raw oscillators, filters and synthetic reverb in NumPy and SciPy, timed to the picture.

04

Picture

Remotion, React and TypeScript for structure; GLSL shaders for light; Three.js for tens of thousands of GPU particles.
